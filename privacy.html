This notice explains how Aurelion Sdn Bhd (“Aurelion”, “we”) collects, uses and protects your personal data when you visit aurelion-earth.com, contact us, or take part in our training. We follow Malaysia’s Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).

1. Data we collect

  • Enquiries: your name, organisation, email, phone or WhatsApp number, team size, preferred timing and what you write to us.
  • Training and HRD Corp claims: participant names, job titles, contact details and attendance records that your company gives us so we can run the course and support its HRD Corp grant application.
  • Assessments: answers to the Leonard Personality Inventory (LPI) and the individual reports produced from them.
  • Feedback: course evaluations, testimonials and, with your consent, photos or video from sessions.
  • Website use: pages visited, clicks, device and browser type, and approximate location, collected through cookies and analytics tools (see section 6).

2. Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare proposals and quotations.
  • To plan, deliver and follow up on training, and to give HR teams the course details they need for HRD Corp.
  • To produce and debrief LPI reports.
  • To improve our programmes and website.
  • To send you updates about our programmes, only if you have agreed. You can opt out at any time.
  • To meet legal, accounting and HRD Corp requirements.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only when needed:

  • Your employer’s HR team, for attendance and grant claims. Individual LPI results are shared only as agreed with participants before the session.
  • HRD Corp, when a grant claim requires it.
  • Service providers who run tools for us, such as WhatsApp (Meta), our online form provider, email, website hosting and analytics. They may store data outside Malaysia. We use providers that protect it to a comparable standard.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

4. Is giving us data required?

No. But without contact details we cannot reply, and without participant details we cannot run a course or support an HRD Corp claim.

5. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry records for up to 2 years after our last contact, and training and claim records for as long as HRD Corp and tax rules require. After that we delete or anonymise them.

6. Cookies and analytics

Our website may use Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand which pages help visitors. These tools set cookies and record clicks and scrolling, without collecting what you type into forms. We respect your browser’s “Do Not Track” setting. You can also block cookies in your browser settings.

7. Security

We limit access to people who need it, use secure (HTTPS) connections and trusted providers, and keep assessment reports confidential.

8. Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or update your personal data, withdraw consent, or stop marketing messages. We may charge the fee allowed by the PDPA for a data access request. Email us and we will reply within 21 days.

9. Other Aurelion services

Aurelion Insights and Life Garden personal growth reports are sold on their own websites, which have their own privacy terms.

Notis Privasi (Bahasa Malaysia)

Notis ini menerangkan cara Aurelion Sdn Bhd (“Aurelion”, “kami”) mengumpul, menggunakan dan melindungi data peribadi anda apabila anda melawat aurelion-earth.com, menghubungi kami atau menyertai latihan kami, selaras dengan Akta Perlindungan Data Peribadi 2010 (PDPA).

1. Data yang kami kumpul

  • Pertanyaan: nama, organisasi, e-mel, nombor telefon atau WhatsApp, saiz pasukan, masa pilihan dan mesej anda.
  • Latihan dan tuntutan HRD Corp: nama peserta, jawatan, butiran hubungan dan rekod kehadiran yang diberikan oleh syarikat anda.
  • Penilaian: jawapan Leonard Personality Inventory (LPI) dan laporan individu.
  • Maklum balas: penilaian kursus, testimoni dan, dengan persetujuan anda, gambar atau video sesi.
  • Penggunaan laman web: halaman dilawati, klik, jenis peranti dan pelayar, serta lokasi anggaran melalui kuki dan alat analitik.

2. Tujuan penggunaan

  • Membalas pertanyaan dan menyediakan cadangan serta sebut harga.
  • Merancang, menjalankan dan membuat susulan latihan, serta memberikan butiran kursus untuk permohonan HRD Corp.
  • Menghasilkan dan membincangkan laporan LPI.
  • Menambah baik program dan laman web kami.
  • Menghantar maklumat program jika anda bersetuju. Anda boleh berhenti melanggan pada bila-bila masa.
  • Mematuhi keperluan undang-undang, perakaunan dan HRD Corp.

3. Pihak yang menerima data

Kami tidak menjual data peribadi. Data hanya dikongsi dengan pasukan HR majikan anda (kehadiran dan tuntutan; keputusan LPI individu hanya seperti yang dipersetujui), HRD Corp, penyedia perkhidmatan kami (seperti WhatsApp, borang dalam talian, e-mel, pengehosan dan analitik, yang mungkin menyimpan data di luar Malaysia), dan pihak berkuasa jika dikehendaki undang-undang.

4. Adakah wajib?

Tidak. Namun tanpa butiran hubungan kami tidak dapat membalas, dan tanpa butiran peserta kami tidak dapat menjalankan kursus atau menyokong tuntutan HRD Corp.

5. Tempoh simpanan

Rekod pertanyaan disimpan sehingga 2 tahun selepas hubungan terakhir. Rekod latihan dan tuntutan disimpan selama yang dikehendaki oleh HRD Corp dan peraturan cukai, kemudian dipadam atau dijadikan tanpa nama.

6. Kuki dan analitik

Laman web kami mungkin menggunakan Google Analytics dan Microsoft Clarity. Alat ini menetapkan kuki dan merekod klik serta tatalan, tanpa merekod apa yang anda taip dalam borang. Anda boleh menyekat kuki melalui tetapan pelayar.

7. Keselamatan

Akses dihadkan kepada mereka yang memerlukannya, sambungan selamat (HTTPS) digunakan, dan laporan penilaian dirahsiakan.

8. Hak anda

Anda boleh meminta untuk melihat, membetulkan atau mengemas kini data anda, menarik balik persetujuan, atau berhenti menerima mesej pemasaran. Fi yang dibenarkan oleh PDPA mungkin dikenakan untuk permintaan akses data. Hubungi kami melalui e-mel dan kami akan membalas dalam masa 21 hari.
