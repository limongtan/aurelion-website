WORLD MENTAL HEALTH DAY · KUALA LUMPUR

Well within. Well beyond.

On 10 October 2026, Aurelion brought EMBRACE to Regus Tribeca, Kuala Lumpur, with a network of partners and supporters. The workshop created space for people to explore well-being, connection and purpose together.

Regus Tribeca was the venue partner. Pong Ent and Hello Family joined as workshop partners, and Popomama as a program partner. The original event poster below credits the wider group of supporters.
