Why do it this way

One evening. Two jobs done.

A normal year-end dinner says thank you. This one also leaves your team with a plan for next year.

Thank them

Real thanks, said out loud

In the Appreciation Circle each person writes a specific thank-you card for every teammate, and the cards are read out at the table.

Plan ahead

A team charter they own

Each team writes its purpose, 3 working agreements and 30-60-90 day actions, presents it, then signs it on the Commitment Wall.

Count it

7 hours of HRD Corp training

The afternoon and the dinner module count as training hours. We collect the attendance, assessments and photos the claim needs.

The day

Afternoon to dinner, 2.00pm – 10.30pm.

Three modules. Every activity ends with a debrief, so the lessons come from your team's own experience.

2.00pm

Welcome

  • Ice-breaker: “Two Truths & a Goal”, and the group's ground rules
2.15pm

Module 1 · Looking Back

Self & team reflection
  • Know Yourself: working-style self-assessment and pair sharing
  • Team Timeline: map the past 12 months, highs and lows, and where the team stands now
  • Blind Build: rebuild a hidden model with a blindfolded builder, then an After Action Review
  • Team Mirror: what to Start, Stop and Continue
4.00pm

Tea break

4.15pm

Module 2 · Moving Forward

Collaboration & growth
  • Trust Walk: guide a blindfolded partner by voice only
  • Feedback that helps: active listening and SBI feedback role play
  • Bridge Builder: two halves of a team build one paper bridge that must meet in the middle
  • Growth plan: each person's 30-60-90 day plan, with an accountability buddy
6.30pm

Break and move to the dinner venue

7.00pm

Module 3 · Reflections Dinner

Celebrate, connect & commit
  • Table Talk cards: mixed-department tables share stories from the year
  • Dinner (main course, 7.30pm)
  • Team Build X Quest: team trivia, drawing, mime and a team cheer
  • Appreciation Circle: a thank-you card for every teammate
  • Team Charter: each team presents, then signs the Commitment Wall
10.15pm

Close

  • Summary, Q&A and one word each: “Tonight I'm taking away…”

Blind Build

One person can see the model. One person builds it, blindfolded.

Everyone else carries the messages in between. In 12 minutes the team finds out exactly where its information gets lost, then talks it through. It's what happens at work every week, only funnier.
