WHAT WE WORK ON

From individual awareness to shared action.

Aurelion is an HRD Corp Registered Training Provider. Each of our HRD courses includes the Leonard Personality Inventory (LPI). Course activities connect the assessment to leadership, team dynamics, cross-department work, or practical AI. We design around what your people need to do differently after the room empties.

Tell us your audience, challenge and timing. We can provide the course information your HR team needs for its claim application.
