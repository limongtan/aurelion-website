How the HRD Corp claim works

Every course on this page is HRD Corp claimable. We run it in-house for your team, and we send the course details your HR team needs for the grant application.

  1. 1

    Pick a course

    Tell us your team, your challenge and the month you have in mind. We send the course details.

  2. 2

    HR applies

    Your HR team applies for the HRD Corp grant before the training.

  3. 3

    We train your team

    Once it is approved, the fee is paid from your levy.

All claims are subject to HRD Corp approval.
