01

What is the LPI?

2,400 years of personality science — with the missing piece finally added.

The LPI maps every person across five forces — Openness, Neutral, Analytical, Relational, Decisive — revealing not just what people do, but the deeper need driving why they do it.7

It yields 26 named personality styles, reads in 13 languages, and has profiled thousands of professionals across Asia, Europe, Australia and New Zealand.7 Where most tools hand you a label, the LPI hands you a working map — of yourself, and of your whole team on one page.

The lineage — 2,400 years, one missing force

~400 BC

Hippocrates observes that people come in four temperaments — the oldest personality framework on record.

~200 AD

Galen names them: sanguine, choleric, melancholic, phlegmatic. The four-corner map is born.

1900s

Modern Western instruments (the DISC family and kin) put the four temperaments to work — but stop at describing behaviour.

2001

The LPI adds the missing fifth force — Openness — grounds it in a holistic model of the whole person, and norms it in Asia.7

The LPI's Openness isn't a corner at all — it sits in the middle of the map as a creative amplifier: high O makes whatever else you have more inventive, and licenses you to roam the map when the team needs it. 03

Most tools stop at behaviour. This one reaches underneath.

The LPI was never a standalone quiz. It is the measurement layer of Dr. Yong's Holistic Model of the individual — which is what separates it from a label-maker: it explains why people do what they do, not just what they do.12

Read it right to left, the way you meet a person: the ONARD behaviour you can see lives in the Body — it's the surface. Driving each visible dial is a core emotional need in the Mind:

O needs Creativity N needs Stability & security A needs Accuracy R needs Recognition D needs Achievement

When a need is fed, the Body shows the strengths column — ideas, calm, precision, warmth, drive. When it's starved, the same dial shows its stress face. Underneath both sits the Spirit layer: meaning, values, wellness — the thermostat of the inner life.

Emotional Excellence (EE) spans all three layers; ordinary EQ only reaches Mind and Body — which is why the LPI is one of the few instruments designed inside a whole-person development model rather than as a labelling exercise.12

Need № 1 · Social-Emotional Security
"Do I belong here? Am I accepted?"

The need to feel you truly belong to the relationships you're in — guarded hardest by the N and A forces. When it's starving: defensiveness, silence, over-compromise, and the quiet exits that blindside managements.12

Need № 2 · Inner Significance
"Do I matter here? Do I add value?"

The need to feel important to others and to add real value — chased hardest by the R and D forces. When it's starving: showboating, credit wars, steamrolling, and burnout heroics that end in collapse.12

"Pay cheques rent behaviour. These two needs buy loyalty."

Every Aurelion engagement uses the LPI to read which need is starving in which person — so development speaks to deeper motivation, and the transformation actually holds.12
