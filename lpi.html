What it is

Five forces live in every person.

The LPI maps every person across five forces — Openness, Neutral, Analytical, Relational, Decisive — revealing not just what people do, but the deeper need driving why they do it.

OOpenness

Curious, inventive, imaginative. Loves trying new things.

Craves creativity
NNeutral

Calm, loyal, a real listener. Keeps the room together.

Craves security
AAnalytical

Precise, careful, systematic. Values accuracy over speed.

Craves accuracy
RRelational

Warm, persuasive, magnetic. Lights people up.

Craves recognition
DDecisive

Fast, bold, result-oriented. Shows initiative.

Craves achievement

Everyone has all five — your top two run the show, and the starved ones run the drama. Feed a force and you get its strengths; starve it and you get its stress face. Openness is the fifth force most tools miss, and the one that measures adaptability in an AI-driven world.
