Monthly plan

RM499 / month

That's under RM34 a reel. Pay monthly, stop any month.

Start with a free sample
  • 15 vertical reels, about 4 a week
  • A short script for each, written by us
  • Captions on screen for people watching on mute
  • Music, cuts and effects
  • Post caption and hashtags, ready to paste
  • Mix any styles above across your 15 reels
  • 1 round of changes per reel

How it works

1Send us 10 to 20 photos or short phone clips a month on WhatsApp. Products, shop, people, anything.
2We write, design and edit your reels in the styles you picked, and send them in batches.
3You post from your own account. Your followers see a shop that's active every week.

Questions

What happens after the free sample?

The reel is yours to post. If you want 15 a month, we can start that week. If not, that's the end of it and we won't keep messaging you.

Will the cartoon look like my real shop?

We draw from your photos, so your colours, products and layout carry over. For product shots where details matter, we use your real photos as they are.

Do the pictures move?

Yes. Every reel has camera movement, cuts, captions and music. Some styles can also have full character animation.

Can I use different styles in one month?

Yes. Mix as many as you like across your 15 reels.

Do I need to film anything special?

No. Normal phone photos are enough. We can start with what's already on your Instagram.

Is there a contract?

No. Pay monthly and stop any month.

Will this get me more followers?

We can't promise numbers. Posting good reels every week is what gives Instagram a reason to show your shop to new people.

Try it on your own shop first

Leave your Instagram ID and email. We'll make one reel from your photos and send it to you shortly, free.
