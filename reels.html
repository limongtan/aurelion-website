Questions

Before you decide.

Anything else? Ask us on WhatsApp ↗

Which option is right for us?

If you pay the HRD levy and have people who can own your content, the course is usually the better deal: your team keeps the skill. If you want reels without spending staff time, choose done for you.

Do my staff need design or video skills?

No. The course starts from setting up the tools and writing the first script. Day 2 uses free AI tools.

What does the third day add?

Day 3 happens after 2 to 3 weeks of real posting. Your team brings its own numbers, learns story videos, makes a week of posts in one sitting, and leaves with a 30-day plan.

Is the coaching required?

No. Growth Lab Plus is optional, for teams that want Andy to review their results every week after the course. It is RM10,499 a month, 3 months minimum, and is not HRD Corp claimable.

What happens after the free sample reel?

The reel is yours to post. If you want 15 a month, we can start that week. If not, that’s the end of it and we won’t keep messaging you.

Will the reels look like my real shop?

We work from your photos, so your colours, products and layout carry over. For product shots where details matter, we use your real photos as they are.

Is there a contract for done for you?

No. Pay monthly and stop any month.

Will this get us more followers?

We can’t promise numbers. Posting good reels every week is what gives Instagram a reason to show your business to new people.
