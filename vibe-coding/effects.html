Special effects, made by the AI

Spinning wheels, cards that fly in, numbers that slam, a screen that shakes. The AI writes the effect as a small web page and turns it into a video. You describe it in plain words, and it builds, checks and fixes it.

Good for: promos, lists, "we've made 10 kinds", prices, counters, before and after. Not for: stories. A story needs real scenes and feelings, not a wheel.

Every video on this page was made this way, by AI, from the prompts below.

Once only

Let the AI set up its video tools

The effect is a web page with animation, rendered to an MP4 by a free tool called HyperFrames. The AI installs it and proves it works with a 3-second test.

Set up

Set yourself up to make video effects with code. Do every step yourself:
install Node.js, ffmpeg and HyperFrames (an HTML-to-video tool, npx hyperframes),
make a folder [Documents/video-effects], and render a 3-second test video to prove it works.
Build every effect as an HTML page animated with GSAP, rendered to 1080x1920 MP4 at 30 fps.
Only stop when I must approve something.
2

The effect

The spinning wheel

  • 0-1 s: cards burst out of the middle into a 3D wheel
  • 1-4 s: slow, fast, slow, while a counter ticks 01 to 10
  • 4-5 s: it lands, the 10 slams, a stamp drops, the screen shakes
  • Whoosh, click and boom sounds, made by the AI too

Spinning wheel

Make a 5-second vertical video with a spinning 3D wheel of cards.
- Cards: [10] cards, one per [kind of video / product / service]: [list the names].
  Pictures: use my files in [folder]. If I have none, ask an image AI for one picture
  per card, with no text in the pictures.
- 0-1 s: the cards burst out of the middle into a wheel, with a whoosh.
- 1-4 s: the wheel spins slow, fast, then slow. A big counter ticks [01 to 10]
  with a click on each number, next to my headline "[We've made 10 kinds of AI video]".
- 4-5 s: it lands on card 1 with a small bounce, the number slams, a stamp
  "[All made with AI]" drops in with a boom and the screen shakes a little.
- My colours: [cream #F7EFE2, brown #3A2A1E, gold #B8761A]. Font: [Poppins].
- Make the whoosh, click and boom sounds yourself.
Before showing me, look at frames every 0.5 s and fix anything that covers
the headline or runs off the screen.

Change it with one line

Make the spinning slower and the cards bigger.

Done: one calm turn instead of two fast ones, cards 30% bigger. The captions now sat on the cards, so I hid them in the first 2 seconds; the headline already says the same words.
3

Any reel

Make it move for real

Words that only fade in look like slides. Real motion is things flying, dropping, slamming and shaking, timed to the voice. Left: before. Right: after.

Real motion

Make every scene of this reel move for real, not only words appearing:
- Every item enters with a move: cards drop and bounce, words spring up,
  the answer slams in with a small camera shake, wrong things shake,
  lists pile up, phone messages pop.
- Moves land on the spoken word (use the voice's word timings).
- Each scene swipes out before the next one flies in.
- No quiet stretch longer than 1.5 s outside the captions.
Then measure it: count the big moves outside the caption area,
and show me before and after side by side.

Motion alone won't make a reel feel different. Pictures, a host with expressions and sound matter more. Give the effects something to move.

4

Put it together

Use it in a promo

  • The wheel spins behind the offer, so the screen never sits still
  • The price card drops on "a month", the price slams on "RM499"
  • Real pictures from your own work, not stock

Promo reel

Make a 20-second promo reel for [business] that uses the spinning wheel.
Facts: use only what is on [my page / this list]: [offer, price, numbers].
Never invent numbers, and never say "all" or "every" unless it is true.
1. Hook: [my strongest finished piece] full screen from the first frame,
   with one line: "[...]".
2. The wheel: [10] of my real [reels / products] spin, then land.
3. Proof: [numbers] slam in one by one.
4. Offer: [price / what they get], with a wheel of [styles / options] behind it.
5. End: "[call to action]" + [link / handle], held for 2 seconds.
Voice: [voice] reading my script, word-by-word captions, a light beat,
and whoosh / tick / hit sounds on every move.
Show me frames of every scene before the final render.
5

Before you post

Ask for an honest check

A good-looking effect can still lose viewers in the first second. Ask for a forecast based on your own past reels, and a second opinion from another AI.

Check before posting

Before I post, check this reel honestly:
1. Watch it frame by frame. Fix text covered by moving things,
   items running off the screen, and quiet stretches.
2. Forecast for my account: 3-second skip %, average watch and shares at 24 h,
   based on my own past reels. Then ask ChatGPT for its forecast
   without showing it yours.
3. If the forecast misses skip under 30% or watch at least half the length,
   don't call it ready: tell me the one change most likely to fix it.

Example: our own promo

Pass the gate?

Not yet. My forecast: 55-70% skip, 4.5-7.5 s watched of 20 s. ChatGPT: 60-75% skip, 2% chance to hit the targets. Our three earlier "made by AI" reels lost 63-77% of viewers in 3 seconds. Fix: open on our best reel full screen, and use the wheel as proof after it.

Learned the hard way: tiny spinning cards on the first frame make people work before they get anything. The wheel works best as proof, after a strong first picture.
