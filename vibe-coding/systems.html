Basics

Website or system?

A website shows things. A system remembers things and does work. The simple test: if it must keep something a person typed in (a booking, an order, an answer), it's a system.

A website

Pages, photos and a little script. Nothing is saved. Cheap, fast and hard to break.

Your computerThe project folder. The AI works here.
→

A system

Same start, plus three new parts: a server that does the work, a database that remembers, and outside services for payments, AI or messages. Each new part means one more account, one more secret key and one more monthly bill.

ScreensWhat people see and fill in.
→
ServerChecks logins, does the work, follows your rules.
→
DatabaseRemembers users, bookings, orders.
→
Outside servicesPayments, AI, email, WhatsApp.

Real example: Aurelion Insights is a system. People answer a questionnaire, AI writes their report, they log in to read it and pay online. Screens, server, database, an AI service and a payment service all work together.

Start small, then grow

  • Version 1 can be a website.Life Garden sells on WhatsApp first: no database, no payment system, live in one afternoon.
  • Add the system parts when people actually use it.Online payment once orders come in. Logins once you have repeat customers.
  • Ask the AI for the plan first.The starting prompt makes it list the parts, accounts and monthly cost before building.
