    05 — The roastery

    Twelve kilos.
    Zero shortcuts.

    01

    Bought by the bag, not the tonne.

    We buy micro-lots direct from eleven farms and pay 40% above fair-trade minimums. When a lot runs out, it's gone — and the next one gets its own label.

    02

    Roasted to a recipe, then tasted.

    Every batch is logged, cupped the next morning and scored. Anything under 84 points becomes the staff coffee. (Sorry, staff.)

    03

    Printed by hand on a two-drum riso.

    Pink and blue, slightly off-register, on recycled stock. Every label is a little different — like every roast.
