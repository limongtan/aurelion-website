The approach

A workshop is a dinner party with an agenda.

Good hosts know who sits where, when to bring out the main course, and how to make the quiet person feel heard. So do good facilitators.

first, the prep

We listen before we design.

Every engagement starts with six short interviews. We find the conversation your team keeps postponing and build the day around it.

then, the room

Structure that feels like freedom.

Clear timeboxes, simple tools, zero jargon. People talk more when they know exactly what the next twenty minutes are for.

and after

The follow-through is the point.

A written recap within 48 hours, owners on every action, and a 30-day check-in to see what actually changed.

We finally said the thing we’d been circling for two years, and nobody left the room upset.Chief Operating Officer, logistics group
