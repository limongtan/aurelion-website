04 · How a project runs

Five stages. No surprises in between.

You pay against finished milestones, never ahead of the work. The schedule below is a sample; your contract sets the real one.

  2. STAGE 01

    Site visit

    Day 1 · ~90 min

    We measure, check wiring, plumbing points and management rules, and listen.

    Sample: free, no obligation
  3. STAGE 02

    3D design

    Weeks 1–3

    Measured plan, 3D views and a material board you can hold. Two revision rounds.

    Sample: design fee, credited if you build with us
  4. STAGE 03

    Quotation

    Week 3–4

    Line-by-line, fixed scope. Every brand, model and thickness written down.

    Sample: 20% deposit on signing
  5. STAGE 04

    Build

    6–20 weeks

    Weekly photo log in a shared chat. One project lead on site, every week.

    Sample: 30% after M&E · 30% after carpentry
  6. STAGE 05

    Handover

    Final week

    Joint defects walk with a checklist. Fixes logged, dated and closed.

    Sample: 15% at handover · 5% after defects period
