No. 04 · House notes

House rules & questions.

Check-in and check-out times

Check-in from 3 pm, check-out by 11 am. Book direct and, if the room is free that day, stay until 2 pm at no charge.

Shoes off inside

Our floor tiles are old and loved, so shoes stay at the door. House slippers are waiting for you.

Stairs and access

Upstairs rooms use a steep traditional staircase and there is no lift. The Kopi Room is on the ground floor for guests who prefer no stairs.

Quiet hours

10 pm to 7 am. The airwell carries sound beautifully, which also means it carries voices.

Children, smoking and pets

Children are welcome; the Airwell Suite and Rooftop Garden suit families. No smoking indoors. No pets, apart from the house cat.

Parking and getting here

No on-site parking; public car parks are a short walk away. Grab from Penang airport takes about 30 minutes. Bicycles are free to borrow.

Cancellation (sample policy)

Free cancellation up to 7 days before arrival. After that, the first night is charged.
