    ■FAQ
    Before you call.
    Straight answers to what procurement and plant managers ask us most.
    
  
    
   
    Q.01How fast can you quote?
    Simple jobs within one working day. Larger fabrication packages within three to five days after the site survey.
    
   
    Q.02Do you work during shutdowns, nights and weekends?
    Yes. Most of our maintenance and tie-in work is planned around your production schedule, including public holidays.
    
   
    Q.03Which areas do you cover?
    The Klang Valley daily, from Port Klang to Rawang. Projects elsewhere in Peninsular Malaysia by arrangement.
    
   
    Q.04Can you work from our drawings, or design for us?
    Both. Send PDFs or CAD files, or we survey and produce shop drawings for your approval before we fabricate.
    
   
    Q.05What paperwork do we get at handover?
    As-built drawings, material certificates, test reports, a method statement and a warranty letter, in one handover file.
    
   
    Q.06Do you offer credit terms?
    Approved trade accounts get 30-day terms. New clients usually start with a deposit on the first order.
