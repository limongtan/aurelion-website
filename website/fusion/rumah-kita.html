    Questions

    Good to know.

    Can’t find your answer? WhatsApp the office and a volunteer will reply.

    Who can come to the centre?

    Everyone. We are a community centre, not a place of worship, and we welcome people of every faith and none, every race and every age.

    How do I get help from the food bank?

    WhatsApp us or walk in on Wednesday or Saturday morning. A volunteer will have a short, private chat with you and register your family. Bring your IC if you have it; if not, come anyway.

    Will I get a receipt for my donation?

    Yes, by email the same day. In this sample we show tax-exempt receipts under section 44(6); check your own organisation’s approval before you promise this.

    Can I donate food or items instead?

    Yes. Dry food (rice, oil, tinned fish, milk powder), school supplies and adult diapers are always needed. Drop them at the office, 9 am to 6 pm.

    Can anyone book the hall?

    Yes: kenduri, birthdays, talks, classes and celebrations of any faith or culture. Alcohol is not allowed. Registered non-profits get 40% off.

    Is there parking?

    There are 30 free bays in front, and street parking after 6 pm. The nearest bus stop is a five-minute walk away.
