Seventeen days

Her baby is dead.

She still won’t let it sink.

And this isn’t the first time.

In 2018, her baby died too.

She carried it for seventeen days.

Then she had a son.

He lived.

In 2025, it happened again.

She’s been here before.

She carries her anyway.

Who told you to be over it by now?
