They walked in a circle for seven days

They walked in a circle for seven days.

Food was right there.

Milo was a good caterpillar.

Eyes on the one ahead.

Never stopped.

On day three, he smelled pine.

Real food, close by.

But nobody else was stopping.

And everyone was working so hard.

That had to mean something.

So he asked the one in front, “Where are we going?”

“No idea. Ask the one in front of me.”

The question went up the line.

Then it came back, from behind him.

“Where are we going?”

There was no front.

Everyone was following someone who was following them.

A French naturalist named Fabre really saw this on a flowerpot.

Seven days, with food close by.

Real life works the same way.

You’re busy.

You’re tired.

Everyone looks like they know where they’re going.

They’re looking at you.

Working hard is your strength.

Overdo it, and you never look up.

On day seven, Milo stopped.

Then he turned toward the pine.

One by one, they turned too.

So who are you following?

Before you follow anyone, ask where they’re going.

Who are you following, and do you know where they’re going?
