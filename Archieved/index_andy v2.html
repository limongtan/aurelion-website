01 · What is the LPI?

Built by a psychometrician.
Proven in boardrooms.

Designed by Prof Dr Leonard Yong, Universiti Malaya. A decade of research, 25 years of deployment, normed in Asia. 50 questions online → a 12-page personal report: strengths, blind spots, stress signature.

Track record
Organisations where the LPI's creator, Prof Dr Leonard Yong, personally consulted and trained leaders — and deployments via Gem Consultancy, our certifying body, for Shell and the Government of Sarawak. Sources: Yong, L.M.S., Emotional Excellence in the Workplace; Gem Consultancy engagement record.
02 · The Five Forces — O·N·A·R·D

Five forces. One map.

OOpennessthe amplifier

Ideas, adaptability — flexes toward what the team needs.

NNeutralthe stabiliser

Harmony, patience — keeps people calm under load.

AAnalyticalthe verifier

Rigour, precision — checks it properly.

RRelationalthe energiser

Warmth, people-energy — wins the room.

DDecisivethe closer

Action, ownership — makes the call.

Everyone has all five — your top two run the show. On one map: who thinks first, who acts first, the chemistry, and the force nobody holds.
